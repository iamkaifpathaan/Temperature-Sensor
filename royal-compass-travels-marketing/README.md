## Before the client call

- [ ] Re-read Section 0 (Project at a glance) and Section 12 (Things we should NOT accidentally promise) in the playbook.
- [ ] Look through royalcompasstravels.com on mobile and desktop. Note 3–5 real issues you have confirmed yourself.
- [ ] Check their Instagram: when they last posted, follower count, what they post, and whether they use reels.
- [ ] **Agree between us** whether the 10 reels are in addition to the 30 posts or part of them. The deck shows them separately.
- [ ] **Agree between us** the payment terms, whether the fee includes GST, and how many revision rounds each item gets (we suggest 2).
- [ ] Decide who leads the call and who takes notes.
- [ ] Open the deck and test screen-sharing it.

- [ ] Within 24 hours of the call, send a written summary covering scope, fee, ad budget, responsibilities, next steps and the list below.

## What to request from Royal Compass before we start

1. **Website access:** a CMS/admin login or their developer's contact, plus hosting access if it's needed for the fixes and the admin lead area.
2. **Domain/DNS:** only if we need it to verify Search Console. Otherwise the client adds the record and we guide them.
3. **Google:** Search Console owned by their Google account, with us added as users. Google Business Profile access is optional.
4. **Meta Business Manager:** partner access for us, and an ad account with **their own payment method** added.
5. **Instagram and Facebook Page:** access through Meta Business Suite, not shared passwords.
6. **Business details:** legal name, address, phone, WhatsApp, email, business hours, and any registrations or affiliations.
7. **Services and packages:** 1–3 priority destinations for Month 1, with itineraries and what is and isn't included.
8. **Pricing and offers** they approve for publishing, with validity dates.
9. **Their actual policies** (cancellation, refund, payment, privacy) for the legal pages.
10. **Brand assets:** high-res logo, brand colours, real trip photos and videos, and testimonials they have permission to use.
11. **10–20 questions** customers commonly ask on calls or WhatsApp.
12. **Target market:** which cities or countries their customers come from.
13. **Lead handling:** who follows up on enquiries, where lead notifications should go, and how quickly they aim to respond.
14. **One approver** for content and creatives, and how quickly they can turn approvals around.
